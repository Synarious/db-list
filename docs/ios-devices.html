Sharing on iPhone, iPad, and Android

Image export is only supported on desktop. To share your results on mobile, open Results and select Share URL (Copy). Paste the link into a message. If copying fails, select Show link and copy the URL manually.

If you opened someone else’s results, copy the URL from your browser’s address bar to share that same checklist.

Find a text download

For a plain-text copy, choose Download TXT on the Results page. Check your browser’s downloads or the Downloads folder in your device’s files app.
