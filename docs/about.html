About and privacy

What is this checklist for?

Deviant Bound’s checklist helps adults discuss interests and boundaries with partners. You can review your choices and download a text file or image to share.

Where are my answers stored?

The app stores answers for this site in your browser profile. Clearing site data can erase them. The app creates downloads and reads backup files on your device. The app does not send ratings to the server. Read aloud uses a locally installed voice, and anyone nearby may hear it.

To keep a copy, use Export backup under Instructions & backups on the checklist or under Answer backup on the results page. The JSON file contains your ratings in plain text. Store it somewhere private.

Open Instructions & backups on the checklist and select Clear saved answers to delete the app’s saved ratings from this browser. Downloads remain until you delete them separately. Age confirmation is stored for the browser tab’s session.
