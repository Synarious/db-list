          How the ratings work

          Each activity direction pairs corresponding roles, such as giving with receiving. The comparison uses supplied genders as checklist defaults for anatomy and fluid roles. If a saved rating falls outside those defaults, check which role applies.

          The one-partner result pairs each person’s rating for the other person’s gender. It checks mutual openness at every interest level. Specific multi-partner scenarios need a shared description of who would participate.

          Pairs involving Let’s talk about it are tentative. Each mutual pair uses the lower interest rating. For example, Favorite with Like counts as Like, and Like with Willing to try counts as Willing to try.

          The circle’s slices include shared boundaries and show the full mix of comparable pairs. The center gives the share of scored pairs that meet the selected interest level. Willing to try paired with Off the table counts as a conflict in Standard and stays outside the score in Relaxed.

          A hard limit remains a boundary at every setting. Agree on consent together before trying anything.
